05 · ABOUT

An academic profile built around chemistry, research and impact.

Dr. Sangay Wangchuk is an academic in the Department of Natural Sciences at Sherubtse College, Royal University of Bhutan, with a research background in analytical chemistry and electrochemical sensing.

He completed a PhD in Analytical Chemistry at Prince of Songkla University, Thailand. His doctoral work focused on functionalized carbon nanomaterials and their applications in environmental monitoring and biomedical analysis.
