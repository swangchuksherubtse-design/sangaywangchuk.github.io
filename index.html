05 · ABOUT

An academic profile built around chemistry, research and impact.

Dr. Sangay Wangchuk is an academic in the Department of Natural Sciences at Sherubtse College, Royal University of Bhutan, with a research background in analytical chemistry and electrochemical sensing.

He completed a PhD in Analytical Chemistry at Prince of Songkla University, Thailand. His doctoral research focused on the development of electrochemical sensing platforms based on functionalized carbon nanomaterials for environmental monitoring and biomedical analysis.

His broader research interests include sustainable nanomaterials, portable and smartphone-integrated electrochemical sensing, environmental analysis, biomedical sensing and forensic electrochemistry.
