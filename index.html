ENVIRONMENTAL · ELECTROCHEMISTRY

Nitrate sensing using Cu₂O@APC modified screen-printed electrodes

Activated porous carbon derived from orange peel combined with Cu₂O for nitrate detection in drinking water, with smartphone-integrated electrochemical measurement.

BIOMEDICAL · PORTABLE SENSING

Uric acid detection using N,S-co-doped carbon nanomaterials

A portable electrochemical sensing platform integrating N,S-CAB-CNTs with a screen-printed electrode and smartphone-compatible potentiostat for uric acid measurement.

05 · ABOUT

An academic profile built around chemistry, research and impact.

Dr. Sangay Wangchuk is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Natural Sciences at Sherubtse College, Royal University of Bhutan. His academic and research interests centre on analytical chemistry, electrochemical sensing, functional nanomaterials and practical analytical technologies.

He completed his PhD in Analytical Chemistry at Prince of Songkla University, Thailand. His doctoral research focused on the development of electrochemical sensing platforms based on functionalized carbon nanomaterials for environmental monitoring and biomedical analysis.

His broader academic work includes chemistry teaching, curriculum development, programme leadership, laboratory development and supervision of research and student projects.
